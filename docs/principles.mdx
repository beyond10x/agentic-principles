# Principle catalog

These are the claims currently worth testing—not eleven commandments. Maturity stays visible because a
useful seed and a supported principle carry very different evidential weight.

:::info Maturity discipline

**Seed** means an observation or synthesis worth investigating. **Candidate** means scope, mechanism,
counter-pressure, operational consequence, and a falsifier are defined. Nothing here has yet earned
**supported principle** status.

:::

## Promotion standard

A principle advances only through reviewable synthesis. It must survive serious attempts at
falsification and gain independent empirical support relevant to its intended domain. A single
incident, benchmark, paper, source, or successful run cannot promote it by itself.
