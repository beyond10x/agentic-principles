# Start here

Agentic Principles asks a practical question: **how can AI agents perform consequential work without
outrunning their authority, evidence, or ability to recover?**

We turn incidents, standards, experiments, transcripts, and live tests into claims that engineers can
challenge and test. The focus is practical automation: software delivery, SRE, customer service, and
the operational work around a SaaS business.

<Admonition type="note" title="Where the research stands">

This is an early research program. The catalog currently contains **one candidate principle** backed by
a mixed-method study and **ten seeds** that still need focused testing. There are no supported
principles yet. That distinction is intentional, and it appears everywhere a claim is shown.

</Admonition>

## Start with the strongest current result

The catalog is ordered by evidence maturity, so the most developed claim appears first. “Candidate”
does not mean settled: it means the claim has a mechanism, scope, counter-pressure, falsifier, and
operational consequence that are ready for further testing.

## How to read an evidence label

| Label | What it means | A responsible use |
|---|---|---|
| **Seed** | An observation or synthesis worth investigating. | Generate a research question; do not turn it into policy. |
| **Hypothesis** | A falsifiable prediction with a proposed mechanism. | Design a test that could distinguish it from a competing explanation. |
| **Candidate** | A scoped claim with evidence, trade-offs, a falsifier, and an operational consequence. | Run independent tests or a bounded product experiment. |
| **Supported** | A claim that survived serious falsification and has independent, relevant empirical support. | Use within its stated boundaries while continuing to monitor counterevidence. |

A claim can also be **challenged**, **revised**, or **retired**. The record remains visible when the
evidence changes.

## What this site does—and does not—claim

The public site is an evidence surface, not a marketing proof. It should make observations,
interpretations, uncertainty, and source material easy to inspect. A polished page does not promote a
seed, a passing test does not establish a universal rule, and a product recommendation is not a
verified product outcome.

For the full mission, scope, and promotion standard, continue to the
[research vision](./VISION.md). To inspect the source or reproduce a result, use the links in each
study or <Link to="https://github.com/beyond10x/agentic-principles">open the repository</Link>.
