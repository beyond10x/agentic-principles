# Research seed: principles for agentic engineering

- Created: 2026-08-25T00:16:13+02:00
- Status: seed, not policy
- Source snapshot: `atlas@25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309`
- Scope: principles for engineering agents and the systems that govern them

## Purpose

This note extracts research hypotheses from the organization described by Atlas. It deliberately
separates observations from inferences: Atlas is one system and one short operational history, so it
can generate candidate principles but cannot by itself validate universal ones.

## Observed system

1. **Responsibility is split by trust boundary.** A metaharness drives the agent loop; the loop runs
   inside a sandbox; model traffic crosses a gateway; identity issues scoped audiences; connectors
   govern external operations; and an event log supplies append-only state machinery. The executable
   engineering specification is also separate from the harness scored against it.
   ([Atlas map, lines 13–23 and 26–40](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/README.md))

2. **Local and cross-cutting rules have different homes.** Component invariants live with their
   component. Disagreements and coordinated changes move to Atlas as cross-repo ADRs. Atlas explicitly
   refuses component-local designs.
   ([Atlas map, lines 42–56 and 80–83](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/README.md);
   [Atlas guidance, lines 3–13](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/AGENTS.md))

3. **Shared bytes are contracts, not text.** A schema identifier, token audience, wire identifier, or
   digest verified elsewhere requires an ordered migration, a new contract version, and a record of
   what shipped.
   ([Atlas guidance, lines 26–33](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/AGENTS.md);
   [ADR 0001, lines 56–65](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/architecture/adr/0001-the-former-org-names.md))

4. **Evidence is part of the work product.** Atlas requires inline sources, distinguishes verified
   facts from inference, and records actual work separately from a component changelog.
   ([Atlas guidance, lines 35–44](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/AGENTS.md))

5. **The system distinguishes durable public state from an in-progress workspace.** The organization
   guard evaluates `HEAD`, while reporting uncommitted differences without treating them as the
   repository's current identity.
   ([Atlas map, lines 59–68](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/README.md))

## Failure evidence

These cases are more useful than the intended architecture because they expose where apparently
reasonable rules failed.

- **A green check was not evidence that the check worked.** Four guards passed because quoting and
  regex mistakes made them assert nothing. The replacement was trusted only after a planted violation
  made it fail. This supports testing a verifier with known-bad input, not merely observing exit zero on
  known-good input.
  ([Work log, lines 61–69](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/log/2026-08-24.md))

- **Correct cleanup destroyed useful provenance.** Roughly forty predecessor links were removed after
  being classified as dead; the commits still existed under a moved repository. The correction
  restored a distinction between removing an obsolete identity and preserving navigability.
  ([ADR 0001, lines 19–37](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/architecture/adr/0001-the-former-org-names.md))

- **Syntactic uniformity can break semantic compatibility.** Old names remain legitimate when they are
  live repository locations, wire identifiers, hash domains, credentials, or historical statements.
  The ADR treats those as migration or preservation problems rather than search-and-replace problems.
  ([ADR 0001, lines 44–82](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/architecture/adr/0001-the-former-org-names.md))

- **Copied governance drifted.** Per-repo brand guards were deleted after copies diverged and several
  silently passed. Atlas now declares one organization-wide fence.
  ([Atlas guidance, lines 21–24](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/AGENTS.md))

## Candidate principles

The following are hypotheses to test, not conclusions.

### P1. Bound autonomy with invariants

Specify the state that must remain true, the capability boundary, and the required evidence. Leave the
agent freedom over reversible implementation choices inside that envelope. Escalation should be tied to
crossing a boundary, not to the mere existence of uncertainty.

### P2. Separate proposer, executor, and evaluator

A claim of success is stronger when intent, execution, and evaluation can be inspected independently.
The same agent may participate in all three, but one opaque transcript should not be the only artifact.
Atlas's separation of protocol, harness, metaharness, and substrate is one concrete design to compare
against alternatives.

### P3. Make effects attributable and replayable

Every consequential operation should leave enough durable evidence to answer: what was attempted, under
which authority, against which starting state, what changed, and which check justified completion.
Replay need not repeat an external side effect; it must be able to reconstruct the decision and verify
the resulting state.

### P4. Test the test

No gate is established by passing compliant input. A new or changed verifier must demonstrate at least
one expected failure against a controlled violation, plus a recovery to green. A verifier without a
known sensitivity is an untested assertion.

### P5. Treat boundary bytes as versioned commitments

If another component stores, hashes, authorizes, parses, or verifies a value, changing it is a migration
over a dependency graph. The safe unit of reasoning is the set of producers, consumers, persisted
instances, rollout order, compatibility window, and rollback path—not the file containing the string.

### P6. Preserve provenance and publish corrections

Agents will make incorrect inferences. A trustworthy system retains navigable evidence, records the
correction beside the original decision, and updates the active rule without rewriting history to look
prescient.

### P7. Place each rule at the narrowest shared scope

A component-specific invariant belongs with the component. A rule shared by several components belongs
at their nearest common governance point. Duplication is not decentralization when copies are expected
to mean the same thing.

### P8. Distinguish proposed, observed, accepted, and committed state

Agentic work moves through states with different authority. A working-tree edit, a passing local check,
a commit, a deployed artifact, and an observed production effect are different claims and should not be
collapsed into “done.”

### P9. Make exceptions explicit and load-bearing

An exception should name the external fact that requires it, the risk of removing it, and the event that
would permit retirement. This makes a residual irregularity inspectable instead of silently normal.

## Tensions exposed by Atlas

Atlas also contains useful unresolved tensions at the cited snapshot:

- ADR 0001 says every repository enforces the brand rule with its own guard
  ([lines 39–42](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/architecture/adr/0001-the-former-org-names.md)), while the current
  guidance says those copies were deleted and only the Atlas guard remains
  ([lines 21–24](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/AGENTS.md)). The likely explanation is policy evolution, but Atlas does
  not state an explicit precedence rule between current guidance and accepted ADR text.
- ADR 0001 says frozen wire contracts are never rewritten
  ([lines 56–60](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/architecture/adr/0001-the-former-org-names.md)), while the later work
  log says frozen bundles were re-rendered under a dated exception
  ([lines 71–75](https://github.com/beyond10x/atlas/blob/25939436b672c02f44734c4ab97f6da314aa5309/log/2026-08-24.md)). This raises a research question: when is an
  “immutable” artifact genuinely immutable, and how should emergency exceptions affect trust?
- `HEAD` is the chosen source for repository identity, but delivery and runtime truth may live in a
  commit, tag, deployed digest, database, or external provider. A general model needs an explicit
  authority for each kind of claim rather than a universal “current state.”

## Research questions

1. What is the minimum machine-readable structure for an invariant: scope, predicate, evidence,
   owner, severity, and exception policy?
2. How can an evaluator be independent enough to catch self-confirming agent errors without requiring
   a completely separate implementation for every check?
3. Which operations require approval, which require notification, and which are safe under bounded
   autonomy? Can this be derived from reversibility, blast radius, authority, and observability?
4. What state model cleanly separates intent, workspace effects, committed artifacts, delivery, and
   observed runtime outcomes?
5. How should contract migrations represent producers, consumers, stored values, compatibility windows,
   rollout order, and rollback as an executable graph?
6. How are exceptions made expiring or reviewable without causing a noisy waiver bureaucracy?
7. How should active policy, accepted decisions, historical logs, and corrective addenda relate when
   they disagree?
8. What evidence bundle lets a third party reproduce a completion judgment without possessing the
   agent's full private context or credentials?

## Proposed validation work

1. Encode the brand-guard failure as a small case study: original rule, false-green mechanism,
   adversarial fixture, corrected verifier, and retained evidence.
2. Encode one wire rename as a dependency graph and test whether P5 predicts every required migration
   step and affected persisted state.
3. Compare at least two agent runs on the same bounded task: one governed by procedural instructions,
   one by invariants plus evidence requirements. Measure intervention rate, correctness, and audit cost.
4. Draft a principle record with fields for statement, rationale, scope, counter-pressure, failure mode,
   executable test, evidence, and known exceptions. Reject any principle that cannot produce a
   falsifiable review question or materially change a design decision.

## Initial thesis

Reliable agentic engineering is not unrestricted autonomy and not exhaustive procedure. It is
**bounded discretion over observable state transitions**: capabilities are scoped, shared contracts are
versioned, consequential effects are attributable, verifiers are themselves challenged, and corrections
remain visible.
