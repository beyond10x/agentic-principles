# Initiative: Agentic principles research program

## Outcome

Evidence-backed principles improve the safety and efficiency of agentic automation in software
engineering, SRE, customer support, and wider SaaS operations, with measurable transfer into
`harness` and `metaharness`.

## Scope

The program includes research questions, source and transcript analysis, controlled evaluations,
live tests within explicit safety bounds, principle synthesis, replication, and product handoff.

## Success

Supported principles predict failures or successes outside the observations that produced them and
lead to product experiments whose safety, quality, cost, latency, or operator-attention effects can
be measured.

## Constraints

Research maturity is evidence-driven. Artifact status, document volume, and persuasive prose do not
substitute for falsification, replication, or observed product outcomes.
