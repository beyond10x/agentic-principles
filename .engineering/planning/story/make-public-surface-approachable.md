## Context

The published site is visually polished, but it assumes readers already understand internal terms such
as operating envelope, safe frontier, and evidence maturity. Its Research entry opens the full vision,
the notebook sidebar exposes a timestamp as a title, and the strongest current result appears after ten
early seeds. A new reader has no short path to understand what the project studies, what is currently
known, or how to inspect the evidence behind a claim.

## Acceptance

A first-time reader can identify the project’s purpose, current evidence posture, strongest result,
maturity vocabulary, and paths to methods and source evidence from human-labelled pages at desktop and
mobile widths without decoding filenames or project-specific jargon.

## Scope

- Add a human-oriented research landing page without duplicating the underlying research corpus.
- Curate document navigation and supply descriptive metadata for published notes and evidence pages.
- Rewrite public entry-point copy and catalog labels in plain language while retaining evidence
  calibration.
- Present the strongest claims before exploratory seeds and make the source path explicit.
- Verify production routes, responsive rendering, keyboard-visible focus, and repository contracts.

## Out of Scope

Changing principle claims or maturity, inventing research findings, changing the scientific workflow,
adding analytics, or replacing the Docusaurus visual system.
